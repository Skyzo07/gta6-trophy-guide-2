    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Vice Got a Price! — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Three stages, in order. Work them in sequence: the story first, the 100% checklist second, the stragglers last.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. Guides report the Chapter 1 balcony meditation may be required, and it is unconfirmed whether it can be done later. Do it the first time it is offered in Chapter 1, and keep a manual save from early Chapter 1 as insurance. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.

      100% Completion & Notes

      The 100% checklist (reported by PowerPyx)

      After the story ends, the checklist appears at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Earlier progress already counts. PowerPyx reports the checklist covers roughly 60% of true completion.
